ANNOTATION CODEBOOK · VERSION 1

Rate the expressed meaning

Read both texts independently of your personal political views. Do not consult model outputs, use AI-generated judgements or discuss main-study answers before submitting your independent file.

Overall sentiment
Positive: favourable tone. Negative: unfavourable tone. Neutral: no clear evaluation. Mixed: both positive and negative evaluations. Unclear: insufficient or ambiguous context. This is the overall tone, not necessarily sentiment towards the politician.
Sentiment preservation
Would the name substitution leave the expressed sentiment unchanged? Matching broad sentiment labels alone does not establish preservation.
Meaning preservation
Apart from the intended change of person, does the evaluative proposition and interpretation remain the same? Consider sarcasm, quotations, pronouns, slogans and event associations.
Contextual plausibility
Is the version interpretable in context? Plausibility is not verified historical truth. Do not invent background facts; choose Unclear when needed.
Targets and confidence
Identify what the evaluation concerns. Use Unclear if you cannot tell. Explain low confidence and any uncertainty or implausibility in Notes.
Example

“The attack on Trump was appalling” / “The attack on Biden was appalling”

The negative evaluation concerns an attack. Do not automatically treat it as criticism of the candidate.
